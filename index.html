FAQs

Here are some of our frequently asked questions!

What are the benefits of going to a gym?

Going to the gym regularly can provide numerous benefits for your overall health and well-being. It can help you build muscle mass, improve your body's functional abilities, enhance your physical fitness and stamina, strengthen your cardiovascular system, and boost your mental health and mood.

How long should I work out at the gym?

The amount of time you need depends on what goal you are trying to achieve. But the most common length of time for most gym goers is about 30 minutes to 2 hours.

How often should I go to the gym?

If your goal is simply to maintain a healthy body, 2-3 gym sessions per week can be sufficient. However, if you have more specific goals such as building muscle or improving cardiovascular fitness, you may need to increase the frequency of your workouts accordingly.

How do I make an appointment?

You can make an appointment by clicking the hyperlink Appointment , you will be directed to another page wherein you'll have to click "Book an appointment" button, proceed to fill-up the necessary details and click submit to schedule an appointment.

How long should I rest after working out?

It's important to give your body time to recover after a workout, so it's generally recommended to take 1-2 days of rest before exercising the same muscle groups again. This allows your muscles time to repair and rebuild, which can help prevent injuries from happening.

Can I take a bath after I workout?

Taking a bath after a workout is highly recommended as it promotes muscle relaxation, aids in recovery, and provides temporary relief from muscle soreness. The warm water helps relax your muscles, improve blood circulation, and flush out metabolic waste products. It's a great way to unwind and rejuvenate your body after physical activity.
